# How is your day? - Daily Tarot Experience

A lightweight, elegant, and immersive web-based daily tarot reading experience. Users can follow their intuition to draw a single card from a full 78-card fan deck to receive personalized guidance for the day.

## Key Features & Interactions
- **Seamless Cover Transition**: Features an aesthetic cover view that smoothly transitions into the main divination interface upon clicking the `Start` button.
- **78-Card Fan Layout**: Utilizes CSS 3D transforms and precise angle calculations to arrange all 78 tarot cards in a graceful, curved arc.
- **Intuitive Single-Card Draw**: Clicking any card triggers a 3D flip animation, and then elevating and highlighting the chosen card.
- **Instant Fortune Interpretation**: Reveals the corresponding card symbol, name, and detailed daily meaning, combining narrative Major Arcana archetypes with elemental Minor Arcana insights.
- **One-Click Reset**: Allows users to return to the cover view and reshuffle the deck to start a new reading at any time.

## Target Audience
Young individuals who are looking for a moment of mindfulness, emotional comfort, or playful psychological inspiration during their busy daily routines or study schedules.
